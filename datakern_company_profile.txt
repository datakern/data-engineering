DataKern Company Profile

DataKern was founded in 2024 to revolutionize data engineering education.
It started as a small community project but quickly grew into a platform serving thousands.
The primary focus is on hands-on, practical learning rather than just theory.

Our flagship course is the AI Engineering Masterclass, which teaches students how to 
build LLM agents, deploy RAG pipelines, and master tools like LangChain and FastAPI.
This course is primarily taught by Uma Kiran Ramadugu, a Data & AI Architect.

DataKern also offers a Data Engineering track covering Azure, PySpark, and Databricks.
Our infrastructure runs heavily on cloud-native technologies and serverless architecture,
allowing us to scale our learning platform globally with minimal downtime.

In 2025, DataKern expanded its offerings to include live cohort-based bootcamps. 
These bootcamps run for 8 weeks and guarantee a portfolio-ready project upon completion.
We partnered with several top-tier tech companies to ensure our curriculum aligns with industry standards.
